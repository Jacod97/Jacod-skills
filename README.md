### write-docs

The `write-docs` skill helps you develop research plans, keep research logs, write reviews of papers and technical concepts, and create general explanatory documents about datasets, libraries, codebases, and tools. Research plans lay out goals, methods, and proposed experiments, while research logs record experiments, results, and progress. Review documents guide readers through the background and reasoning needed to understand a topic, and general explanatory documents organize information for later reference. All four types share common writing rules, with guidance tailored to each purpose, and focus on accurate, clear, logically structured content rather than visual design.
